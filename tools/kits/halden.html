Welcome to Halden Furniture

Halden Furniture was founded in 1987 and since then we have been making quality furniture for your home. Our products are made from the best materials and our team of experienced craftsmen make sure that every piece meets our high standards of quality. We offer a wide range of chairs, tables, sofas, lighting and storage solutions for every room in the house. Visit our showroom or browse our online shop. Delivery is available to all of Scandinavia and Europe. Please contact us if you have any questions about our products or services, our customer service is happy to help you Monday to Friday from 9 to 17.
